Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Celli Calendar is a private, personal integration used by Kai to connect a self-hosted OpenClaw environment with Google Calendar.

On this page

What we use Where it is stored What we don't do Your control Contact

What Google Calendar data is used for

When Kai authorises access, Google Calendar data is used solely to perform user-requested calendar operations. This may include reading calendar details, creating events, updating events, or deleting events.

Celli Calendar does not take independent actions. Operations are performed in response to a user-directed task through OpenClaw.

Where it is stored

OAuth credentials and tokens are stored privately in the user's self-hosted OpenClaw environment. This website does not provide a public account system and does not store calendar data.

What we don't do

  • We do not sell Google Calendar data.
  • We do not share it for advertising.
  • We do not use it for unrelated purposes or model training.
  • We do not offer public user accounts.

Your control

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account permissions page. Revoking access prevents future authorised requests from using Google Calendar through Celli Calendar. You can also remove the integration from the self-hosted OpenClaw environment.
