Small, intentional, useful

Your calendar, with a little more context.

Celli Calendar is not a public service or a social app. It is a private integration made for Kai's own workflow.

01

Connect

OpenClaw connects to Kai's Google Calendar through Google's authorisation flow.

02

Ask

Kai gives a clear instruction, such as checking availability or adding an appointment.

03

Act

Celli Calendar reads, creates, updates, or deletes events only to carry out that instruction.

The short version

A focused connection, not a data business.

Calendar data is used solely for user-requested calendar operations. It is not sold, shared for advertising, or used for unrelated purposes or model training.
